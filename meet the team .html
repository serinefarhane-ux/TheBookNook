OUR PURPOSE

More than just a website.

Book Nook was created to bring everything a reader needs into one welcoming space. From keeping track of what you're reading to discovering your next favorite book, our website is designed to make the reading experience easier, more organized, and more enjoyable.

We wanted to create something that reflects the feeling of getting lost in a good book — while adding a little technology along the way.
